How Kickstopper Works

Boycotts fail because acting alone feels pointless. Kickstopper fixes that.

  1. Someone names a target and a demand. "Amazon must pay warehouse workers $25/hr minimum."
  2. You pledge a specific action. "I will cancel Prime and not shop on Amazon for one year."
  3. Your pledge is conditional. It only activates if the campaign hits its threshold — say, 100,000 people.
  4. If the threshold is met, everyone acts together. If not, nobody is obligated.

No more lonely boycotts. No more wondering if anyone else cares. Move together or don't move at all.
